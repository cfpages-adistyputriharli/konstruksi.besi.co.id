# Article Catalog — konstruksi.besi.co.id

Scope: 120 non-geographic article briefs across 20 steel-construction topics. The catalog converts the audited site into a connected knowledge system; it is a planning inventory, not permission to mass-publish.

Domain boundaries: educational pages explain decisions, evidence, risks, and lifecycle tasks. Existing `/desain/`, `/konstruksi/`, `/rab/`, `/las/`, and `/fabrikasi/` routes own commercial conversion. Project-specific calculations, drawings, welding procedures, fire engineering, lifting plans, statutory approvals, and compliance certification remain with qualified professionals. Related owned domains may cover the same query independently.

Catalog statistics: 20 topics; 120 briefs; exactly 6 briefs per topic; 14 P0, 40 P1, 46 P2, and 20 P3 briefs. No location-swapped brief is included.

Priority legend: P0 = bounded first cluster; P1 = next authority/commercial-support layer; P2 = specialist depth; P3 = long-tail/reference after evidence capacity exists. Wave 1 is limited to 14 assets; later waves require review of quality, indexation, query separation, and commercial outcomes.

## First-wave publishing standard

Each article must link to its parent hub, prerequisites, and next-stage assets; service links appear only after the informational task is complete. Before Wave 2, evaluate valid indexation, impressions by unique intent, checklist/tool use, engaged visits, qualified contacts, answer rate, conversion/commission, collected revenue, and query-to-page cannibalization. Rankings alone do not justify expansion.
